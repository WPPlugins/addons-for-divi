== Description ==

Tired of complex modules, poor performance, and expensive add-ons for essential features, only to get meh results?

Introducing Divi Torque, It is an ultimate all-in-one addon for Divi Page Builder. Under the hood, it includes the most comprehensive modules, such as Image Carousel, Flip Box, Team Module, Business Hour, etc. Why do you need multiple plugins? Where is everything under the one hood?


<strong> FREE MODULES AND COUNTING </strong>

1. Divi Image Carousel - Easily create an interactive image carousel with customizable settings to showcase your images in a visually appealing way.

2. Logo Carousel - Display multiple brand or company logos in a carousel format, perfect for showcasing sponsor logos or partners.

3. Logo Grid - Showcase brand or company logos in a grid layout, with customization options such as the number of rows and columns.

4. Divi Team Module - Showcase your team on your website with images, titles, and descriptions for each member, along with social media links.

5. Divi Testimonial - Display customer testimonials on your website with customizable settings, such as text alignment and typography.

6. Divi Alert Module - Display notices, warnings, and attention texts on any website with an alert module that offers different alert styles and customization options.

7. Divi Compare Image - Display a before and after image, perfect for showcasing the transformation of a product or service.

8. Divi Business Hour - Showcase your office hours for clients and visitors, with customizable settings such as the days of the week and time intervals.

9. Torq Info Card - Demonstrate products or creative posts using a beautiful combination of elements, such as images, text, and buttons.

10. Divi Contact Form 7 - Style existing Contact Form 7 forms to match your website's design with customizable settings such as color and typography.

11. Torq Gradient Heading - Create a gradient heading in a few seconds with customizable settings for gradient colors and typography.

12. Divi Icon Box - Display a short brief about the services you provide with customizable settings for icon size and color.

13. Divi Blurb - An extended version of Divi's default blurb module with additional customization options for text and icon size.

14. Torq Image Scroll - Show images in a way that scrolls horizontally or vertically with customizable settings for animation speed and image spacing.

15. Torq Progress Bar - Showcase skills and other progressive information with customizable settings such as bar color and percentage.

16. Divi Twitter Feed - Fetch specific Twitter account feeds on your web pages with customizable settings for the tweet limit and display options.

17. Torq Twitter Carousel - Show Twitter feeds in a carousel format with customizable settings for the tweet limit and display options.

18. Divi Video Modal - Create a video lightbox popup in Divi builder for YouTube and Vimeo videos with customizable settings for video titles and captions.

19. Divider Module - Separate your section with fancy divider styles, such as dots or lines, with customizable settings for color and thickness.

== Installation ==

> Note: This plugin works with Divi. Make sure you have [Divi Theme](https://www.elegantthemes.com/) installed.

1. Upload the plugin folder to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install the plugin through the WordPress plugins screen directly.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress
3. You can type "Torq" on your module tabs within the Divi editor and all the available modules will appear.
